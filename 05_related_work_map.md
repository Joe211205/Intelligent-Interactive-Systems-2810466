# Related-work map and gap sentence

## Themes (two or three; name each in four words)

### Theme A — Explainability aids error-checking
- Janssen, M. et al. (2020): Explainable AI combined with experience helps decision-makers detect incorrect algorithmic suggestions, though not all mistakes are caught.
- Vössing, M. et al. (2022): Transparency features improve human-AI collaboration by helping users understand system behaviour and identify when outputs may be unreliable.

### Theme B — Explainability supports trust resilience
- Xu, M. and Wang, Y. (2024): Explainability mitigates trust decline after AI errors have occurred, reducing decision regret and raising willingness to reuse AI systems.
- Shin, D. (2021): Explainability and causability increase user trust and acceptance of AI systems, especially after observing system behaviour.

### Theme C — 
-  Govea, J., Gutierrez, R. and Villegas-Ch, W. (2024): Explainability increases transparency and precision in recommendation systems, helping users understand why recommendations are produced.

## Gap sentence

These sources establish A (explainability helps users notice some errors), B (explainability supports trust resilience after errors) and C (explainability improves clarity in recommendations), but none shows whether increasing explainability or transparency improves people's ability to detect mistakes in AI recommendations during decision-making; that is the gap this project addresses.

### Three tests
- every phrase in the first half carries a theme letter;
- the second half is a question a study could answer within the eleven-week module;
- it is a gap in the literature, not a gap in my reading. A theme called 'other papers' means the sorting has not been done.
