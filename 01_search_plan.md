# Search plan

Problem sentence:  People trying to complete tasks with AI assistants struggle to detect mistakes because the assistant does not explain how it reached its conclusions.

## Concept blocks (two or three terms each; take synonyms from the first abstracts you read)

| Block |    What it covers      | Terms                                                   |
|   A   | the person's behaviour |error detection, verification, checking AI decision      |
|   B   | the system's property  |explainability, transparency, reasoning clarity          |
|   C   |       the setting      |AI assistant, decision support system, intelligent agent |

## One natural-language question (for tools that read questions)
Does increasing the explainability or transparency of an AI assistant help people find mistakes in its recommendations?
## One Boolean query (for tools that match strings: OR inside a block, AND between blocks)
("error detection" OR "checking AI output" OR "verifying recommendations") AND ("explainability" OR "transparency" OR "reasoning clarity") AND ("AI assistant" OR "decision-support system" OR "intelligent agent")
A block with no synonyms is probably your project's name, not a concept.
